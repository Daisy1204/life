CERTIFICATION / QUALITY

Quality needs
evidence.

We distinguish packaging claims from documentation that can be verified. A badge should say who issued it, what it covers and when it is valid.

READING QUALITY MARKS

Look beyond
the icon.

Current packaging imagery includes GMP, third-party testing, natural-formula and FDA-facility references. Until valid certificates or reports are supplied, these are not described as independently verified product certifications.

Important: FDA does not approve dietary supplements or issue GMP compliance certificates for supplement facilities. Facility registration is not FDA approval or certification.
01 / GMP

Good manufacturing practices

GMP is a manufacturing and quality-management framework. It is not, by itself, proof that every production batch has been certified.

02 / INDEPENDENT TESTING

Third-party testing

A meaningful claim should identify the independent laboratory, batch, test date, methods and tested items.

03 / NATURAL FORMULA

A formulation description

“Natural” or “plant-inspired” is not a certification and does not automatically mean organic, non-GMO or free from all other ingredients.

04 / FDA WORDING

Use accurate wording

Claims should not say “FDA-certified facility”. Any registration wording must stay within the evidence supplied.
