LIFE · INGREDIENT STUDY

The language
of one sachet.

These are the ingredients listed in Life’s proprietary blend. We describe their source and scientific category without turning research context into a medical promise.

01 / SEED

Black cumin seed

Nigella sativa

A plant seed long used in food and herbal traditions; research often discusses its naturally occurring thymoquinone.

02 / BERRY SEED

Black raspberry seed

Rubus occidentalis

Berry seed research commonly describes polyunsaturated fatty acids, tocols and polyphenolic compounds.

03 / GRAPE SEED

Grape seed

Vitis vinifera

A source of plant polyphenols; proanthocyanidins are among the groups commonly studied in grape seeds.

04 / CARBOHYDRATE

D-ribose

D-ribose

A five-carbon sugar found in biochemical pathways related to nucleotides and cellular energy metabolism.

05 / POLYPHENOL

trans-resveratrol

trans-resveratrol

A plant-derived stilbenoid polyphenol; trans is one of the commonly described isomeric forms of resveratrol.
